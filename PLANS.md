# Path B build plan

## Winning shape

Path B will demonstrate one complete, believable story: Maya, a sophomore computer science student who works 20 hours a week and must remain at least half-time, does not pass a once-yearly prerequisite. She sees the exact dependency cascade, chooses what to protect, compares viable recovery paths, and leaves with one useful advisor question.

The product promise is narrow: **show what broke, what remains possible, and what each choice protects before a disruption becomes a derailment.** Academic facts come only from typed fixture data and deterministic functions. Claude may explain computed results, but cannot create or change requirements.

## Verifiable milestones

1. **Design and runnable foundation**
   - Produce a complete desktop and mobile concept for the primary student loop.
   - Establish an intentional design system and a minimal React application with lint, typecheck, test, and build commands.
   - Evidence: the first screen explains the disruption problem in under 15 seconds and the app runs locally without credentials.

2. **Deterministic resilience engine**
   - Model courses, prerequisites, term availability, the baseline plan, student constraints, disruptions, and source/assumption metadata with explicit schemas.
   - Compute the failure cascade and at least two materially different, viable recovery paths from the same facts.
   - Evidence: tests cover prerequisite propagation, annual offering timing, protected-priority tradeoffs, and invalid inputs.

3. **Complete student loop**
   - Let a judge trigger Maya's disruption, inspect the causal cascade, choose a protected priority, compare paths, and prepare an advisor next step.
   - Make changed, blocked, moved, and protected plan elements visually distinct without relying on color alone.
   - Evidence: the default flow completes comfortably in two minutes on desktop and mobile, by pointer or keyboard.

4. **Claude explanation boundary**
   - Add one server-only endpoint that accepts validated deterministic scenario summaries and returns a validated explanation, tradeoffs, next steps, and advisor question.
   - Provide a complete deterministic fallback for missing keys, timeouts, upstream errors, and malformed model output.
   - Evidence: the client never receives the API key or prompts; fallback and malformed-output tests pass; the UI labels facts, assumptions, and AI interpretation distinctly.

5. **Product resilience and polish**
   - Cover loading, empty, recoverable error, reduced-motion, narrow-screen, and malformed-AI states.
   - Review the real implementation for architecture correctness, comprehension, accessibility, security, and responsive quality; fix evidence-backed issues.
   - Evidence: lint, typecheck, unit/component tests, build, browser flow, mobile pass, and security review all complete without an unresolved demo blocker.

6. **Judge-ready submission**
   - Publish clear setup/deployment instructions, fixture disclosures, architecture notes, a 500-word-or-less submission draft, and a rehearsed 90-120 second demo path.
   - Evidence: every judging-dimension claim points to something visible in the app or repository; the final no-key production build is deployable and the final audit has evidence for all five dimensions.

## Explicit non-goals

- A general degree planner, degree audit, transcript importer, or institutional integration.
- An open-ended chatbot or AI-authored academic requirements.
- Many shallow disruption types; the polished prerequisite-failure story is the demo.
- Student accounts, persistence, collaboration, advisor messaging, or sensitive student data.
- Predicting financial-aid decisions; the demo exposes a synthetic half-time assumption and directs Maya to a human.
- Three-dimensional, decorative, or hover-only visualizations that slow comprehension.
- Unsupported claims that the fixture represents a real institution or that Path B replaces advising.

## Decision test

Keep a change only if it improves at least one official judging dimension and preserves the two-minute causal story, deterministic source of truth, and human handoff.

# Stellic Pathfinders Challenge — build rubric

Source checked August 11, 2026: https://www.stellic.com/pathfinders

## Official judging dimensions
The challenge states that five dimensions are weighted equally:

1. Does it solve a real student problem?
2. Is it original?
3. How much could it help students if it scaled?
4. The design and experience.
5. How well it is built.

Treat each dimension as 20% of the build target. A feature that does not strengthen at least one dimension should face a high bar for inclusion.

## Submission constraints
A complete submission requires:
- title and category;
- a 500-word write-up describing what was built, the problem, and who it is for;
- a two-minute demo video hosted on YouTube, Vimeo, or Loom;
- a working link a judge can actually open (live URL, Figma, or GitHub); and
- a list of every tool used, including AI tools.

Submissions close August 21, 2026.

## Path B implications
- Primary category: Overcoming Obstacles. Secondary: Degree Planning & Discovery.
- The product must visibly solve disruption/friction, not simply duplicate degree planning.
- Originality comes from degree-plan resilience: stress-testing a plan, exposing dependency cascades, and comparing paths based on what the student wants to protect.
- Scalable impact should be demonstrated through a reusable deterministic scenario model, not claimed through unsupported institutional integrations.
- Design should make causal impact and tradeoffs understandable faster than prose alone.
- Build quality should be visible through deterministic academic facts, explicit assumptions, AI schema validation/fallbacks, responsive/accessibility work, tests, and safe secret handling.
- Technical complexity is not itself a judging criterion. Prefer credible student value over unnecessary infrastructure.

## Final checkpoint
Before submission, run `product_judge` against all five dimensions and require a concrete evidence item in the working app or submission for every major claim.
